Two formats

Raw records when you need detail. A PDF when you want readability.

CSV export

Creates a CSV with category, start date, end date, value, and unit. Useful for spreadsheets, backups, personal archives, and your own analysis.

PDF summary

Creates a shareable Health Summary Report with metric cards, up to four rendered monthly trend charts, selected date range, data-coverage information, and source-row count.

Privacy & limits

Your Health data stays on your device unless you choose to export it.

Export Health requests read access only to the Health categories used by the app. OpsOh does not collect your Health data. Export files are created on-device, and you decide where to save or share them.

Important: Export Health can only include records Apple Health makes available on the device for the selected range. Missing or unrestored historical records may not appear. The PDF summary is for personal review and organization; it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or health recommendations.
