Can I export Apple Reminders from my iPhone?

Yes. Export Reminders lets you choose a reminder list on iPhone and export its reminders into a file you can save or share.

Can I export Apple Reminders to CSV?

Yes. Choose CSV when you want a spreadsheet-friendly export for records, review, planning, or reporting.

Can I export Apple Reminders to JSON?

Yes. JSON is available when you want a structured-data export for archiving or other data workflows.

Why export reminders instead of leaving them only in Apple Reminders?

An exported file gives you a portable copy for backup, records, spreadsheet review, reporting, sharing, or reorganizing your task data.

Who makes Export Reminders?

Export Reminders is developed by OpsOh, an independent studio that builds focused iPhone utilities for everyday workflows.
