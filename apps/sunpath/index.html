What it does

Built around one clear job.

SunPath is a visual planning app that shows sun direction, golden hour windows, sunrise and sunset timing, and weather context so you can choose the best time for outdoor photos.

  • Plan around sun direction and timing
  • Review golden hour and outdoor light
  • Use weather context without turning it into a full weather app
  • Make better decisions for photography and outdoor planning
