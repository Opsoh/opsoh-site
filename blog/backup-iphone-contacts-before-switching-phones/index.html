Why a separate contacts backup is worth making

Cloud sync is useful for keeping contacts available across devices, but it is not the same as having a file you control. Sync depends on account settings, network access, and everything going right during the transfer.

A file export gives you a snapshot of your contacts at a specific point in time. If contacts look different after the switch — missing, duplicated, or showing the wrong details — a saved CSV or VCF file lets you see exactly what was there before.

CSV or VCF — which format to use before a phone switch?

VCF is usually the more practical format when you may need to import contacts into another app or address book later. Most contacts apps can import VCF files directly.

CSV is more useful when you want to review your contact list in a spreadsheet — to check for missing information, duplicates, or inconsistencies before the switch.

For a thorough backup, saving both formats covers both needs.

What to do before switching phones

Export your contacts, save the file to a location you can access after the switch (cloud storage, email to yourself, or an external folder), and open the file once to confirm it includes the contacts you care most about.

If your contacts are spread across multiple accounts — iCloud, Google, Exchange — check whether the export captures all of them, or whether you need to export from each account separately.

Quick checklist

  • Export contacts before starting the phone switch — not after.
  • Use VCF if you may need to import contacts into another app later.
  • Use CSV if you want to review the contact list in a spreadsheet.
  • Save the backup somewhere accessible after the switch.
  • Open the file to confirm the contact list looks right.

Frequently asked questions

Should I export contacts before switching phones?

Yes. It is a practical safety step that gives you a separate copy of your contacts before the sync or transfer process starts.

Which format is best before switching phones?

VCF is best if you may need to import contacts later. CSV is best for reviewing your contact list in a spreadsheet before the switch.

Does exporting contacts replace iCloud sync?

No. Exporting creates a file-based backup. iCloud sync keeps contacts connected across devices. Both serve different purposes.

Where should I save the contact backup?

Save it somewhere you can access after the switch — cloud storage, email, or a computer — rather than only on the old phone.
