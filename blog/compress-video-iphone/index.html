Why compress video on iPhone?

Smaller videos are easier to send through email and messaging apps, faster to upload to websites or forms, and take up less storage space on your phone. This is especially useful for reference clips, casual sharing, documentation, or any video where the original quality is more than necessary.

Quality and size tradeoffs

Compression reduces file size by making tradeoffs — the smaller the output file, the more likely some visual detail is reduced. For most sharing and documentation purposes, the compressed version is perfectly usable. Always keep the original if quality matters.

When compression is worth it

Compress videos before: sending large clips that exceed messaging limits, uploading to web forms that have size restrictions, archiving many recordings where individual file size adds up, or sharing videos where delivery speed matters more than maximum quality.

Quick checklist

  • Decide whether you need the original quality.

  • Check the compressed video before deleting the original.
