What calendar export means

A calendar export creates a file from your calendar events. Instead of only viewing events inside the Calendar app, you get a saved file that can be stored independently, shared as a document, reviewed in a spreadsheet, or imported into another calendar system.

This is different from syncing. Sync keeps your calendar updated across devices. Export creates a separate, standalone file.

CSV or ICS — which should you choose?

CSV is the right choice for spreadsheets and readable records. It turns events into rows and columns — useful for sorting, filtering, reviewing, or keeping an archive you can read without a calendar app.

ICS is the right choice for calendar apps. It preserves events in a format that Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, and most other platforms can import directly.

Common reasons to export your iPhone calendar

People export calendars for: personal records and archives, business documentation, backup before a device or account change, sharing a schedule as a file rather than a live calendar invite, moving events to a different calendar platform, and travel or project planning records.

Quick checklist

  • Decide why you need the export and what you will do with the file.
  • Use CSV for spreadsheets and readable records.
  • Use ICS for importing events into another calendar app.
  • Select the calendar and date range carefully.
  • Save and verify the exported file before relying on it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I export my iPhone calendar?

Yes. Export Calendar lets you save iPhone calendar events to CSV or ICS files directly from your phone.

What is the best format for calendar export?

CSV for spreadsheets and records; ICS for importing into another calendar app.

Is exporting the same as sharing a calendar?

No. Sharing gives someone else access to a live calendar. Exporting creates a file copy of events at a point in time.

Why would I export calendar events?

For backup, records, account changes, device switches, or keeping a calendar archive outside the Calendar app.
