Studio

Independent apps built with care.

OpsOh is an independent app studio. OpsOh builds focused apps and games around clear ideas — exporting data, organizing photos, handling files and documents, planning outdoor light, reviewing Health data, or simply playing something thoughtful and fun. OpsOh uses a clean, recognisable mark to represent apps that solve practical problems, feel polished, and stay easy to understand. The logo works at small sizes while the OpsOh name remains readable and accessible. Each app begins with one specific job, not a vague feature list.

OpsOh identity

How OpsOh builds

Practical apps, clear workflows, less noise.

OpsOh apps are built around everyday tasks that deserve to be simpler, clearer, and easier to trust.

→

One clear job per app

Each app is designed around a focused outcome: export a file, rename media, clean storage, prepare a PDF, review selected Health data, plan a better moment, or play a focused game.

↗

Practical workflows

The apps support real tasks people actually run into — records, backups, sharing, organization, document prep, and planning.

⌁

Platform-native feel

OpsOh favors lightweight, understandable interfaces that feel comfortable on the devices they are built for.

○

No unnecessary accounts

When an app can do its job locally, users should not need to create an account just to complete a simple task.

◌

Private by design

OpsOh does not need users to hand personal files, calendars, photos, contacts, reminders, PDFs, or videos to OpsOh to use these tools.

↔

Connected knowledge

Apps, guides, bundles, and videos link together so visitors can understand the workflow, not just the download button.
