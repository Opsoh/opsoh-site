Before contacting support

A little context helps us understand the issue faster.

  • Include the app name you are asking about.
  • Describe what you were trying to do and what happened.
  • Mention your platform and OS version if the issue seems device-related.
  • Never send private files, contacts, calendars, photos, PDFs, or videos unless you intentionally choose to share them.
